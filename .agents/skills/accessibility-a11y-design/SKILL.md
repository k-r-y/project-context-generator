# Accessibility (a11y) Design

## When to use this
> Commonly paired or confused with: accessibility-testing, usability-testing. See those skills if this one doesn't match the task.

Use when ensuring a UI is perceivable, operable, and understandable by users with visual, motor, auditory, or cognitive disabilities. This skill applies when working in the **UI/UX Design** domain.

## How it works
Incorporates WCAG guidelines into color contrast, typography, and layout.

**Why it matters:** Ensures the product is usable by individuals with visual, motor, or cognitive disabilities.

## Steps
1. Maintain a minimum color contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text.
2. Ensure all interactive elements are fully navigable and operable via keyboard only.
3. Provide meaningful aria-labels and alt text for all non-text content.

## Never do this
- Do not rely solely on color to convey critical information or state.
- Do not remove focus rings (outline: none) without providing a highly visible alternative.
- Do not trap keyboard focus inside modals or dropdowns.
