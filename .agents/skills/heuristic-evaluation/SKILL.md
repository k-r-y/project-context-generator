# Heuristic Evaluation

## When to use this
> Commonly paired or confused with: usability-testing, accessibility-testing. See those skills if this one doesn't match the task.

Use when conducting an expert audit of an existing interface to surface usability problems quickly without recruiting users. This skill applies when working in the **UI/UX Design** domain.

## How it works
Reviews the interface against established usability principles like Nielsen's 10 heuristics.

**Why it matters:** Identifies severe usability flaws early in the design cycle without needing users.

## Steps
1. Evaluate the interface against Nielsen’s 10 Usability Heuristics systematically.
2. Assign severity ratings to every identified issue to prioritize engineering efforts.
3. Conduct reviews with at least 3-5 independent evaluators to catch 75%+ of usability problems.

## Never do this
- Do not treat heuristic evaluations as a replacement for actual user testing.
- Do not log vague issues without referencing the specific violated heuristic.
- Do not evaluate the system based on personal aesthetic preferences.
